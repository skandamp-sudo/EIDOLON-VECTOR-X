# Scientific and operational limits

- The archived SciFact evaluation has 16 represented claims among 164. Three 16D rank-three IDs changed across original processes. Raw historical scores are unavailable. The alignment replay identifies a one-ULP mechanism. This experiment established no steering benefit.
- QASPER v2 is a small within-paper retrieval experiment on a published dev split. Family separation uses paper IDs and normalized titles. Unknown MiniLM pretraining overlap, possible related papers and duplicate boilerplate preclude a blind-test claim. BM25 has access to all paragraphs. Encoder abstentions remain in labeled retrieval denominators.
- Authors explicitly named sections. The derived method/results labels measure section membership and cannot establish independent expert judgment about methodology, population, causality or clinical applicability. Absolute protected-axis movement and changes in retrieved results sections are different measurements. Matching conditions on nontrivial feasible gain and can exclude difficult queries.
- PCA fidelity describes sampled neighborhoods in each encoder's own frame. Coordinates and learned gains have different meanings across encoders. Development latency uses three abstracts. Peak RSS includes dataset parsing, fitting and evaluation. These measurements cannot predict multi-tenant serving capacity.
- Public deployment is designed for the calibrated inference API and static research reports. Private datasets, live research indexes and optional weights stay local. Host-specific CPU packaging has only been exercised on Intel macOS. Resource ceilings and actual trial eligibility are account-specific.
- Only three of 839 evaluation paragraphs qualified under the frozen methodology-heading rule. They occurred in three papers and none appeared in the unsteered or governed top three of either new encoder. Changing that rule after seeing these data would require a new experiment.

The verified public prototype uses a Vercel Hobby frontend and a Railway trial backend. The service settings showed a 1 GB memory ceiling and 2 vCPU plan limit. Trial credit and duration are finite. The serving basis is fitted on the deployment platform and has a different identity from the local evaluation basis. Live hosted inference uses the calibration model. The four-encoder measurements remain available as static evidence. Desktop Chrome and Pixel 7 emulation were exercised. Physical devices and Safari were not tested.
